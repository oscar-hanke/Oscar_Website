Resume Keyword Matcher - Python-based personal project

A command-line tool that compares a resume against a job description, reporting skills the job looks for, which ones the resume demonstrates, and which ones are missing. I built it after learning that applicant tracking systems screen resumes by keyword. The interesting problem was precision by matching against a curated skill vocabulary rather than filtering out stopwords and fluff, and handling terms like C++ where standard word boundaries fail. Take a look at my README for more on my design choices: GitHub

Supernaturals Talent Agency - Business proposal, UBC-V COMM 396

A proposal for an agency that represents Indigenous talent to lead educational and community-engaged events at public facilities across Metro Vancouver. Grass Field Management System - DBMS, UBC-V CPSC 304 (team of three)

A reservation and maintenance system for municipal sports fields in which we incorporated relational database theory. Park managers can observe and query the status and analytics of parks, resolve active issues, and create private reservations. I designed ER diagrams mapping out entities, their keys, attributes, and relationships. I also drew out and normalized functional dependencies to follow BCNF and 3NF. Finally, I implemented the delete, query, and nested aggregation functions. Code available on request
